## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AgriConnect is a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers. Farmers can list their products, set prices and receive orders through the platform.

How it addresses the problem:

It reduces the dependency on middlemen, helping farmers earn better profits while consumers get products at reasonable prices.

Innovation and uniqueness:
- Direct farmer-to-buyer connection.
- AI-based demand forecasting.
- Smart delivery route planning.
- Transparent product pricing.

## Technical Approach
Technologies to be used:
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript – Frontend.
- React.js – User interface.
- Python and Flask – Backend.
- MySQL – Database.
- Machine Learning – Demand prediction.
- Google Maps API – Route planning.
Implementation process:
1. User registration.
2. Farmers upload products.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
AgriConnect is technically and practically feasible because it uses commonly available technologies such as web development frameworks, databases and machine learning libraries.

The platform can initially be developed as a website and later expanded into a mobile application.

We can start by connecting a limited number of farmers and buyers in a particular region. Once the platform becomes successful, it can be expanded to other districts and states.
2. Potential challenges and risks
Some challenges that may arise during implementation are:
- Farmers may have limited knowledge of digital platforms.
- Internet connectivity may be poor in certain rural areas.
- Transportation costs may affect product prices.
- Agricultural product prices may change frequently.
- Maintaining product quality during transportation can be difficult.
- Accurate demand prediction requires sufficient historical data.
3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges
To overcome these challenges, we propose the following measures:
- Provide a simple and user-friendly interface with regional language support.
- Allow FPOs and local agricultural organisations to help farmers register.
- Partner with local transportation providers.
- Regularly update product prices based on market information.
- Introduce product quality details and basic verification.
- Start AI predictions using available market data and improve accuracy as more data becomes available.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Potential impact on the target audience
AgriConnect mainly focuses on farmers, consumers, retailers, wholesalers and Farmer Producer Organizations.

The platform can help farmers reach more customers without depending entirely on traditional intermediaries.

Small-scale farmers can also get an opportunity to sell their products to bulk buyers and retailers.

Consumers can discover products from nearby farmers and compare prices before purchasing.
